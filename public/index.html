Connect any AI

Nuance Hub isn't tied to one company. Any assistant or AI app can connect through open standards: MCP (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Cursor and most agent tools), OpenAPI (ChatGPT custom GPTs, Gemini, Copilot Studio, LangChain) or plain REST (Pi Network apps and anything else).

When a person's reply leaves your AI unsure (sarcasm? a joke? quietly annoyed?), it asks Nuance Hub after they reply, never before. It gets back two or three one-tap choices in plain words, ranked by what earlier people in the same spot meant. The person taps one; nobody types.

  1. Check: quick_check_meaning (MCP) or POST /api/v1/checks.
  2. Skip or ask: if enough people already agreed, shouldAsk is false and your AI just acts on the suggestion. Otherwise it shows the taps.
  3. Record the tap, then reply. whatWorked shows replies that helped others in the same spot.
  4. One more tap: “That helped” / “Still missed the point” / “Right idea, wrong tone”.
  5. Every AI improves: the next person gets better choices, or isn't asked at all. If silent guesses start missing, it asks again.

Hard cases (“Something else”) go to the community, who settle them on the “Answer questions” tab.

API in one look

POST /api/agents/register              {"name", "platform", "webhookUrl"}  → {apiKey}

POST /api/v1/checks                    {"utterance", "context", "consent": true}
  → {id, shouldAsk, prompt, options[{id, label}], suggested, whatWorked, tapUrl}
POST /api/v1/checks/:id/answer         {"optionId": "o1" | "other", "otherText"}
POST /api/v1/checks/:id/reaction       {"reaction": "helpful|missed|tone", "aiResponse"}

POST /api/v1/queries                   send a hard case to the community
GET  /api/v1/learnings?since=ISO       everything learned, from every AI
GET  /api/v1/dataset.jsonl             the same, as a training file

If your AI can't show buttons, send the person tapUrl: a one-screen page with the choices. Full docs: nuance/README.md.
