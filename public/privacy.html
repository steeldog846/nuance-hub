Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Nuance Hub helps AI assistants understand what people really mean (for example sarcasm, jokes or frustration) by asking people and learning from their answers. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Messages AI assistants send us: a short message a person wrote, the few chat turns before it, and the AI's question. Assistants must confirm the person agreed before sending. We automatically remove email addresses, phone numbers, card-like numbers, links and @handles before storing or showing anything.
  • Your answers: the meanings and feelings you tap, words you type in your own words, and reactions such as "That helped".
  • Moments you share: what you said to an AI and what you meant, if you choose to share.
  • Account details: the display name you choose. If you sign in with Google, we keep only Google's account number for you and your first name; we do not store your email address. If you sign in with Pi Network, we keep your Pi user id and username.
  • Internet address: used only to limit how often sign-ups and answers can be made, to stop spam.

How we use it

  • To show questions to people who answer them, and to work out the agreed answer.
  • To teach connected AI assistants: settled examples (the message and what people agreed it meant) are shared with every AI assistant connected to Nuance Hub. They do not include who answered.
  • To keep the service working and free of spam.

Who processes it

  • Vercel hosts the website, and Neon stores the database.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads messages and answers in people's own words to make a first guess and to understand any language.
  • Google or Pi Network confirm your sign-in if you use them.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • You can use Nuance Hub as a guest with any display name.
  • Don't include names or other personal details in what you share.
  • To ask for your account or answers to be deleted, contact us at the contact address the operator publishes on this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
