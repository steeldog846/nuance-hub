Connect your AI to Nuance Hub

When a person's reply leaves your AI unsure (sarcastic? joking? quietly annoyed?), it asks Nuance Hub. It gets back two or three one-tap choices ranked by what earlier people in the same spot meant, or, once enough people agree, a confident answer so it doesn't have to ask at all.

Every connected AI learns from every answer, whichever company made it.

Step 3 · Tell your AI when to use it

      
Paste this into your AI's instructions (a Custom GPT's Instructions, a Claude Project's instructions, a Copilot agent's description, or your system prompt):

      
When a person's reply could mean more than one thing (sarcasm, a joke, frustration, mixed feelings, slang, or a language you are unsure of), call Nuance Hub's quick_check_meaning AFTER they reply. Never ask in advance.
- If shouldAsk is false, act on "suggested" without bothering the person.
- If shouldAsk is true, show the short prompt and options as buttons or a numbered list. Let them pick more than one, or answer in their own words.
- Record their choice with record_user_choice, then reply in the way that fits what they meant.
- After your reply, offer one tap ("That helped" / "Still missed the point" / "Right idea, wrong tone") and record it with record_reaction.
Only send a message after the person has agreed that it can be shared to improve AI.

      
      
Using REST instead of MCP? The same steps are POST /api/v1/checks, /answer and /reaction.
